Why we made it

Made for people who can't use a mouse

EyeSync is for people living with paralysis, or with a disability or injury that makes a mouse and keyboard hard or impossible to use. A webcam, a little head movement, a smile and voice typing help make a computer easier to use.

Read why we made EyeSync

What it does

Hands-free control from one app

Steer with your face

The calibrated model maps your face to the mouse pointer. Smile for left click; open your mouth for right click.

Voice typing

Close both eyes for one second to start or stop offline voice typing. A beep confirms each change. There is no session time limit.

Tuned to you

On first launch, a nine-dot calibration trains the pointer for your face, camera and screen. Calibrate again from the app whenever needed.

Ready from a shortcut

Run setup once. The desktop shortcut starts EyeSync directly, including calibration on the first launch. Python is bundled.

How it works

Up and running in three steps

  1. 1

    Download setup

    Download EyeSync-Setup.exe for Windows and open it.

    Download EyeSync
  2. 2

    Install the shortcut

    Choose the Desktop or another shortcut folder, then click Install. No Python installation is needed.

  3. 3

    Open and calibrate

    Click the new EyeSync Standalone shortcut. On the first launch, follow the nine calibration dots. The app then starts.

You'll need Windows 10 or 11, a webcam and a microphone for voice typing.

Privacy

Private by design

  • Face tracking and voice typing run on your PC.
  • The optional browser demo uses your camera only after you choose to start it.
  • The website has no account, dashboard, analytics or advertising.
  • No camera picture or dictated words are uploaded to this site.

Accessibility

Built for hands-free use

  • Large buttons and strong focus outlines.
  • Light and dark themes.
  • Reduced-motion support and screen-reader text.
  • Keyboard controls stay inside EyeSync, including a movable on-screen keyboard.

Updating

Download the newest setup file and run it again. It keeps the calibration and settings stored in the EyeSync Standalone folder.

Private and separate

The installer uses its own EyeSync Standalone folder and shortcut. It does not install or update the original EyeSync application. Face and voice processing stay on your PC.

Why we made it

Everyone deserves to use a computer.

We made EyeSync for people who can't use a mouse or keyboard: people living with paralysis, or with a disability or injury that makes moving their arms and hands hard or impossible. Using a computer shouldn't depend on your hands.

The problem

A computer is how we learn, work, talk to the people we love, find information and get everyday things done. Almost all of it is built around two hands on a mouse and a keyboard.

For someone who can't move their arms or hands, or can only move them a little, that can shut the door on all of it. Tools that help do exist, but special hardware can be expensive and hard to get.

About 1 in 6 people worldwide lives with a significant disability, according to the World Health Organization.

Our answer

EyeSync turns an ordinary webcam into a mouse. Small head movements steer the cursor, a smile left clicks, an open mouth right clicks, and a one-second eye closure toggles offline voice typing.

It's free, it needs nothing but a webcam, and the camera picture never leaves your PC. We wanted something anyone could start using today, with what they already have.

Who it's for

For anyone a mouse leaves out

Living with paralysis

People who can move their head but not their arms or hands, for example after a spinal cord injury.

Conditions that affect movement

People with conditions such as ALS (motor neurone disease), multiple sclerosis, muscular dystrophy or cerebral palsy, or after a stroke, for whom a mouse is hard or impossible to use.

Injury, pain and tremor

A broken arm, repetitive strain, arthritis or a tremor. For a few weeks, or for good.

Families and carers

Help set it up once, and the person you care for can use the computer on their own.

What we promise

  • Free. No price, no subscription, no ads.
  • No special hardware. The webcam you already have is enough.
  • Hands-free from start to finish. Move the pointer, click, and type with your voice.
  • Private. Your camera picture, and what you say stay on your PC.
  • Fitted to you. A nine-dot calibration trains the pointer for your face and camera.
  • Easy to reach. Big buttons, strong contrast and calm screens on this website, made for a head pointer.

Is EyeSync right for you?

EyeSync works best if you can move your head a little, even slightly, and smile or open your mouth to click. You'll need a Windows 10 or 11 computer with a webcam. Someone may need to help with the first install.

EyeSync is not a medical device. If it doesn't suit you, an occupational therapist or an assistive technology specialist can help you find what does.
